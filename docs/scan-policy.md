# Scan Policy

Scruto Lite includes basic scan policy controls to reduce misuse and unintended scanning.

The public scan policy principles include:

- The user must confirm authorization before starting an assessment.
- Local, internal, or protected targets may be restricted.
- Certain domains and target patterns may be excluded.
- The assessment flow is designed for basic public website review only.
- Scruto Lite is not intended for unauthorized testing.

For security reasons, the complete abuse-prevention logic, internal validation rules, allow/block lists, and enforcement details are not disclosed in this public repository.
